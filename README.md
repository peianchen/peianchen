# Pei An Chen | 陳沛安

**Embedded biomedical sensing · Research instrumentation · FPGA systems**

[Portfolio](https://peianchen.github.io/peian-chen/en/) · [繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Email](mailto:chen861019@gmail.com)

I am an Electrical Engineering Ph.D. student at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Taiwan, with expected graduation in June 2027. I develop sensing devices, firmware and applications around experimental measurement and control needs.

My work connects physical signals with usable data: acquisition timing, device communication, application state, storage and analysis. Project documentation records the design choices and distinguishes software checks, simulation and physical measurements.
